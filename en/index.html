Syria's property market, in one place and in plain words

Balkoun is a Syrian property site that gathers apartments, houses, villas, land and shops for sale and rent across every governorate: Damascus and its countryside, Aleppo, Homs, Hama, Latakia, Tartus, Daraa, Suwayda, Deir ez-Zor, Hasakah, Raqqa, Idlib and Quneitra. Every listing shows the title-deed status (green deed, court ruling, shares and more) and the area's average price per m² over the last month, so you know whether a price is fair before you call.

Owners and agencies publish for free and without commission, with photos or video, and buyers and tenants contact them directly by phone or WhatsApp. For Syrians abroad: browse in Arabic, English or German, prices in dollars, and a practical guide to buying from abroad and transferring ownership.
